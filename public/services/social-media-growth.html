FAQ

Social Media Growth questions, answered.

Which platforms should we be on?

The ones your customers use. For most consumer brands that is Instagram and YouTube; for B2B it is usually LinkedIn. We recommend a focused set rather than spreading thin.

How often will you post?

It depends on the plan and platform, typically three to five posts a week on the main channel plus stories. Consistency matters more than volume.

Who creates the content?

We do: strategy, design, video and copy. We may ask you for product access, a short shoot or team input so the content stays authentic.

Do you run paid social as well?

Yes. Our digital marketing team runs Meta and LinkedIn ads, and boosting the best organic posts is often the fastest way to grow.

How do you measure success?

Reach, engagement rate, follower growth, saves, shares, profile visits and the leads or sales social brings in, reported monthly.
