Overview

More than a logo: the feeling you leave behind.

A strong brand makes every other channel cheaper. Ads get recognised faster, sales conversations start warmer, and customers pay for the confidence a clear brand gives them. We start with strategy — who you serve, what you stand for, why you are different — and design from there.

The result is a complete identity system: logo suite, colour, typography, imagery and tone of voice, documented in guidelines and templates your team can use without calling a designer for every post.

What's included

Branding, end to end.

  • Brand strategy and positioningAudience, competitors and promise, distilled into a positioning your whole team can repeat.
  • Logo designA primary logo, secondary lockups and an icon, designed to work from a favicon to a billboard.
  • Visual identity systemColour palette, typography, imagery style and graphic elements that make you recognisable.
  • Messaging and tone of voiceTagline, key messages and writing rules so every caption and email sounds like you.
  • Brand guidelinesA practical guide showing how to use every element correctly, with do and do-not examples.
  • Packaging and printLabels, packaging, brochures, business cards and signage that carry the identity offline.
  • Social media brand kitProfile assets and editable post, story and highlight templates.
  • Brand refreshModernising an existing brand while keeping the equity customers already recognise.

FAQ

Branding questions, answered.

How long does a branding project take?

A complete identity usually takes three to six weeks, depending on the number of deliverables and review rounds. A logo-only project can be faster.

What will we receive?

A logo suite in vector and web formats, colour and typography specifications, brand guidelines, templates for everyday use, and all editable source files.

Can you refresh our existing brand?

Yes. We often modernise an existing identity while keeping the elements customers already recognise, so you gain clarity without losing equity.

How many logo concepts do we see?

We present a small number of considered directions, each shown in real applications, then refine the chosen one with you.

Do we own the final designs?

Yes. Full ownership of the final logo and identity passes to you on completion, along with all source files.
