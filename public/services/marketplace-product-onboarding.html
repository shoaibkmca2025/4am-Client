Overview

From documents to first order, handled.

Selling on marketplaces means a different registration, catalog format and rulebook on every platform. We help brands, manufacturers, startups and SMEs get through seller registration and verification, then build listings that meet each platform's guidelines and rank in its search.

After go-live we keep your catalog accurate, prices and inventory in sync, and your listings, brand store and marketplace ads working harder, with one team managing every account.

FAQ

Marketplace Product Onboarding questions, answered.

How long does onboarding take?

The timeline depends on the marketplace and document verification process. Most platforms complete onboarding within a few business days after successful verification.

What documents are required?

Requirements vary by platform but generally include GST registration, PAN, bank account details, address proof, business registration, and product-specific licenses such as FSSAI where applicable.

Can you onboard products to multiple marketplaces?

Yes. We can onboard your products across multiple marketplaces simultaneously to maximise your online reach.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes. We offer catalog management, account maintenance, listing optimisation, advertising support and marketplace growth services.

Can you run ads on the marketplaces?

Yes. We set up and optimise sponsored product ads, deals and promotions on Amazon, Flipkart and other platforms to grow sales after launch.
